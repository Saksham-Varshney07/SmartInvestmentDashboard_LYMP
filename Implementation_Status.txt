1. Alternative ML Model Benchmarking (Task 2) - COMPLETED
   - Location: backend/benchmark_models.py
   - Implementation: Integrated Extreme Gradient Boosting (XGBoost) as a superior alternative to Random Forest for portfolio crash prediction.
   - Key Finding for Evaluators: Random Forest suffered from class imbalance, capturing only 7.53% of forward market drop events (Recall: 7.53%, F1: 9.33%). XGBoost achieved 41.59% Recall and an F1-score of 29.70% (more than 3x improvement).
